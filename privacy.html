Privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This is a static portfolio website. The short version: it collects nothing about you.

What this site does not do

There are no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers on this site. The fonts, images and videos are served from this site itself, not from third-party networks. The portrait in the hero reacts to your cursor inside your browser only; your cursor movements are never recorded or sent anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data (for example IP addresses in server logs) to serve and protect the site, under GitHub's own privacy statement. I do not receive those logs.

Links out

Project and channel links lead to other websites (GitHub Pages sites and YouTube). Those sites run under their own privacy policies.

Email

If you write to me, I keep your email so I can reply and keep track of the conversation. I do not sell email addresses or add them to marketing lists.
